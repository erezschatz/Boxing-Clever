# Answers to the open questions, 2026-11-03

## Context

Erez, 2026-11-03, in conversation, answering the open questions after
task 03:

1. **Deleting a finalised fight** is allowed but heavily guarded: "this is a
   finalised fight, are you sure?", then a dialog showing what the
   fighters' records will be reset to, with accept all, delete the fight
   and keep the records, or cancel back to the fight. Deleting fights never
   deletes the event, even the last fight: a fight entered under the wrong
   event (Dec 12 in Las Vegas instead of Dec 14 at MSG) is deleted and
   re-entered, and the event stays. An event is deleted only by deleting
   the event.
2. **The sheet's 8- and 7-point rounds** are 9-point rounds with 1, 2, 3…
   points deducted; 10–8 as points does not happen. He may have coloured
   them to tell knockdown from foul. If there is no other option, mark
   each deduction "either general or KD"; about 95% are knockdowns.
3. **Which import wrote a bout** does not matter.
4. **Catchweight** does not exist: there is a contracted weight, and a
   fighter can miss weight, which should be marked and highlighted.
5. **TBRB rank** only matters if the fighter was ranked at the time of the
   fight; it is the last ranking prior to the fight, weekly or monthly,
   however old; otherwise unranked.
- **Technical decisions:** the judges score the round the fight was
  stopped in (10–10 if it ended at once), and on a technical decision that
  score counts. That corrects D20.
- **RTD:** "it's usually 'the match ended at 3:00 of round number X'";
  "those are really insignificant and most sites just write them as TKO".

"File everything, either answer the questions or add the new ones, nothing
exists in the chat."
